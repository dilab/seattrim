SeatTrim Marketing site design system · v1.2 · Tailwind CSS · no JS

Quiet software for seats nobody is sitting in.

SeatTrim scans your Zoom account for licenses no one is using and helps you downgrade them safely — so you stop buying new seats and pay for fewer at renewal. This system covers the marketing site only: nav, hero, feature grids, how-it-works, proof, closing CTA and footer. Soft, airy and calm — cool grays, glassy white cards, pastel light, one accent blue.

09

Voice & principles

Calm, plain, a little dry. SeatTrim talks like a careful colleague, not a dashboard.

Copy rules

  • —Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and nav. Full stops on headlines.
  • —“You” and “your account”. SeatTrim says “I” only inside product messages.
  • —Lead with the number, then the reassurance: “76 seats. Nothing changes until you approve.”
  • —Say “downgrade” and “seat”, never “optimize” or “rightsizing”. No exclamation marks, no emoji.
  • —Headlines split in two: the point in ink-900, the relief in ink-400.

Do / don’t

↳ Approve 76 downgrades
Fewer seats. Same team.
Renewal in 18 days
OPTIMIZE LICENSES NOW!
Supercharge your SaaS spend 🚀
